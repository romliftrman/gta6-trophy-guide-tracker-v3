Roadmap to the platinum

Work the phases in order. Story trophies cannot be missed — play the plot and they land. The only things that need care are the five flagged missables (see the Missables tab) and the long grinds, which are cumulative: chip at them between missions instead of leaving them all for the end.

    Missable trophies — flagged from the official descriptions

    Five trophies are flagged possibly missable. The flag is a caution drawn from the wording of the official description (a one-time choice, a second-chance moment, a one-off event, or a partner / location that the story may take away). It is not a confirmed missable: the game is days old and no public guide is complete. Before you finish the story, check the cited guides in the Sources tab for the latest word on each flag — and do the flagged ones early, while the moment is still in front of you.
